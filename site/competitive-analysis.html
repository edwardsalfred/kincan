05 — Patterns of the Top 10%

Three habits separating the winners.

01

Years in business, anchored above the fold.

Adcock says "Since 1973" in the hero. Premier says "Founded in 1988." Jones says "48 years." The Pine Belt market trades on longevity. Kincan's "40+ years combined experience" belongs on the homepage hero, not buried in About.

02

One primary CTA, repeated five times.

Adcock uses "Get Started." Premier uses "Request a Free Quote." The top three repeat one phrase 4-6 times across the page. Kincan currently rotates between "Learn More," "Contact us," and "Submit." Pick one, repeat it, pin the phone in the nav.

03

Real Mississippi photography, no stock.

Adcock and Outdoor Living lead with actual finished Pine Belt pools. The bottom-half sites use stock imagery and it shows. Kincan already owns the red-umbrella pool, five before-and-after case studies, and a working water feature. Promote them.

04

Trust badge cluster.

Master Pools Guild, BBB, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, Google. The badge wall is real and it converts. Kincan already qualifies on HomeAdvisor, Houzz, and Google. Build the visible cluster.

05

Geography as a moat.

The top three name 8-15 specific Pine Belt towns. Kincan serves 15. That's the largest stated coverage area in the field. Render it as a visible service map, not a hidden list.

06 — Recommended Design Direction

What we will build.

Palette

Three of five top competitors use a water blue. Outdoor Living differentiates with sage. ASP uses red. The opportunity is a deep, refined pool cyan as the primary and Kincan's heritage sunset red as a sparing accent. This honors the existing logo while owning a more confident water position than Adcock's tealer cyan or Premier's navy.

Typography

Heading: Fraunces. A confident, warm serif. Reads as premium without going stiff. Body: Inter. Quiet, technical, easy on mobile.

Photography

Lead with the red-umbrella pool and the five before-and-after case studies. Self-host everything in site/assets/. No stock photography on the homepage, ever.

Animation

GSAP + ScrollTrigger. A reserved hero slot for a future scroll-driven 3D pool asset. Section reveals on enter. Parallax on the geography map. Counter animation on the "40+ years" and "$450" numbers (years are static, not animated).

What to avoid

Em-dashes in copy. Generic stock photography. Three competing CTAs. Buried phone number. Hidden pricing. The "franchise" voice from Premier and ASP.
