## 4. Patterns of the Top 10%

Every winning site in this niche does these things. Lower-scoring sites skip them.

1. **Years anchored above the fold.** Adcock says "Since 1973" in the hero. Premier says "Founded in 1988." Jones says "48 years of experience." The Pine Belt market trades on longevity. Kincan must do the same. **"40+ years combined experience"** belongs on the homepage hero, not buried in About.

2. **Real Mississippi photography.** Adcock and Outdoor Living lead with actual finished Pine Belt pools. The bottom-half sites use stock imagery. Kincan already has the right portfolio. It just needs to be promoted.

3. **One primary CTA, repeated.** Adcock uses "Get Started." Premier uses "Design Your Pool." Both repeat the same phrase 4-6 times. Kincan currently rotates between "Learn More," "Contact us," and "Submit." Pick one and repeat.

4. **Award or trust badge cluster.** Master Pools Guild, BBB, HomeAdvisor, Houzz. The badge wall is real. Kincan already has HomeAdvisor + Houzz + Google. Build a visible cluster.

5. **Service area pride.** The top three name 8-15 specific Pine Belt towns. Kincan has 15 towns. Render them as a visible service map, not a hidden list.

6. **Phone number, persistent.** All five top sites pin the phone in the top-right corner of the nav. Mississippi pool buyers call. Kincan's `601.336.0545` should sit in the header on every breakpoint.

## 6. Visual Identity Benchmarks

What the leaders use:

| Brand | Primary color | Secondary | Aesthetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adcock | Aqua/teal `~#1d8aa6` | White, charcoal | Lifestyle, generous whitespace |
| Premier | Navy `~#13355e` + cyan accent | White, gold | Corporate-modern, franchise polish |
| Jones | Blue `~#1a5fa5` | White | Dated, mid-2010s template |
| Outdoor Living | Sage green `~#7ba37a` | Cream, brown | Earthy, lifestyle-curve aesthetic |
| ASP | Brand red `~#c2272d` | Navy, white | Corporate locator polish |

**Insight:** Three of five use a water-blue as primary. Outdoor Living differentiates with sage (and looks the freshest of the field). ASP uses red — same as Kincan's current logo — but reads corporate and templated.

**Recommendation:** Lean into a **deep, refined pool cyan** (`#0E7DAB`) as the primary. It owns "water" without copying any single competitor's exact tone. Keep the heritage **Sunset Red** (`#C8442C`) as an accent for CTAs and badge highlights so the existing logo still reads as in-brand. This is the cleanest visual position in the field.
