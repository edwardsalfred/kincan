## 7. Brand Refinement Strategy

The existing site leans on a red-and-white wordmark over generic stock photography. The rebuild will:

- **Promote water-blue to the primary brand color.** Pool service is a water trade; the visual language should be water. Red moves to accent (CTA outlines, hover states, badges) so the heritage carries forward without dominating.
- **Preserve the existing logo.** No re-logo. The PNG drops in as-is on a light surface; on dark sections we'll display the same PNG with a subtle drop shadow rather than inverting it (no white-tile risk).
- **Lean into the actual portfolio.** The before/after shots and the red-umbrella pool are far stronger than any stock imagery they currently use. Build the hero around real Hattiesburg work.
- **Tighten the geography story.** 15 Pine Belt towns is a competitive moat. Make the service map visible and proud.
